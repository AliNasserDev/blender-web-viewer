    Supported formats

    .blend is parsed fully client-side (js.blend). Meshes, materials & lights are imported; modifiers, particles and packed textures are not evaluated. For full fidelity use Blender’s glTF exporter (.glb). Also supported: .glb / .gltf / .obj / .fbx / .stl / .ply / .dae — drop extra files together to resolve textures & .bin.
